What this shows

This is the capsule membrane's stress field, drawn on its undeformed (flat) reference shape. It does NOT deform — think of it as a fixed map: each triangle sits where that patch of membrane starts, and its colour tells you how hard that patch is being stressed right now in the live SOFA simulation.

Watch the deformation in the SOFA window; watch where the force concentrates here.

Colour = σ₁ (max principal stress)

σ₁ is the largest in-plane tension in that triangle. blue = relaxed … red = peak. The scale (bottom-left) auto-fits the current peak; Scale switches to a fixed ceiling. Grey = a degenerate triangle (collapsed/inverted) where σ₁ is not physical.

Hover a triangle

Shows σ₁, radius r, area ratio, the σ₁ direction, and the crack class. A crack would run perpendicular to σ₁ (Rankine). Comparing that to the radial direction:

  • CIRCUMFERENTIAL — the good, curvilinear tear (stays a circle).
  • RADIAL — the dangerous one that runs out to the periphery.
  • oblique — in between.

The hovered triangle draws its σ₁ line (white) and the crack line (dashed red).

Timeline — drag freely, review after

You don't have to watch while you drag. Every frame is recorded. The bar at the bottom is ◉ LIVE by default (follows the newest frame). Drag the slider or press ▶ to scrub back through history and hover any past moment; Space or ◉ returns to live.
The full field is also written to scenes/stress_log.jsonl (one JSON frame per line) for offline analysis.
